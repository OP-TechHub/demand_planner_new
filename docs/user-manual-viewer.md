# Viewer — User Manual

**Oceanpick Demand Planner**

As a **Viewer** you have full read access to the plan's results but cannot change
any inputs. This manual covers signing in, reading each output, and switching
between plans.

---

## 1. Signing in

1. Go to the app URL and sign in at **`/login`** with your email and password.
2. Forgot your password? Click **Forgot password?**, enter your email, and follow
   the link we send you.
3. New accounts must be **approved by an administrator** before they can sign in.
   If you signed up and see an "awaiting approval" notice, wait for an admin.

The **plan selector** in the top bar shows which plan you're viewing. By default
you see the org's **Live plan**. Everything on every page reflects the selected
plan.

---

## 2. What you can do

- View every **Output** tab (below).
- Switch the active plan with the top-bar selector.
- **Compare plans** side by side.
- Export any table you can see to **CSV**.

## What you cannot do

- Edit programs, demand, harvest, or buckets.
- Create scenarios or plans, save inquiries, or run a recalculation.
- Access **Admin** pages.

If you need a change made, ask a Planner, Contributor, or Admin.

---

## 3. Reading the outputs

All figures come from the last time the plan was **recalculated** — shown as
"last computed" on the Dashboard. If it looks stale, ask someone with edit rights
to recalculate.

- **Dashboard** — headline totals (demand, fulfilled %, revenue, margin) with a
  **time-range filter**, plus charts: monthly demand vs. fulfilled, revenue &
  margin trend, active vs. pipeline split, and top customers by shortfall.
- **Annual Summary** — yearly roll-up of the monthly figures.
- **Program Fulfilment** — per program, how much of its demand is met.
- **Open to buy** — spare whole-round capacity per size bucket and month
  ("Unallocated WR"), what pipeline inquiries have consumed ("Allocated with
  inquiries"), and an inquiry-fulfilment grid. Hover a shaded cell for its
  breakdown.
- **60-Month Summary** — the full month-by-month picture.
- **Revenue & Cost** — money view, with a status filter (Active / Pipeline /
  Combined).
- **Fulfilment Optimizer** — suggestions for improving fulfilment (read-only view).
- **Inquiries** — the register of inquiries raised (filter by date and creator).

**Colour cues:** on the Demand Plan, green = active programs, amber/yellow =
pipeline. Cells shaded green/red indicate fulfilled vs. short; hover for the split.

---

## 4. Comparing plans

**Scenarios → Compare plans** lets you pick any two plans (A and B) and see the
differences in settings, programs, demand, harvest, and the output totals. Useful
for "what changed between last month's plan and this one".

---

## 5. Getting help

- **Wrong or stale numbers?** Ask an editor to **Recalculate** the plan.
- **Can't see a plan you expect?** It may be someone's private sandbox, or you may
  not have been granted access — ask an Admin.
- **Account issues** (locked out, wrong email): contact an Admin.
